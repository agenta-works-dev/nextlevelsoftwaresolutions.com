What is processed, and where?

Reading a file, displaying its source, answering questions, comparing values, and applying profiles happen in your browser. Live interpretation sends selected text and rendered pages through our server to the AI provider stated before you continue.

Our application does not persist uploaded documents, interpretations, or profiles on the server. Processing uses temporary memory. Provider retention policies still apply and are linked in the processing notice. Minimal usage records enforce the free limit.

For public live processing, Cloudflare Turnstile checks for automated abuse after you choose AI processing. Cloudflare receives browser and network metadata under its privacy policy. The local review and recorded AI sample demonstrations do not load this verification service.

What stays after I leave?

Session documents and readings stay in this page’s memory and are released when you remove the session or close the page. A profile is saved in this browser only when you choose “Save profile”. Removing session information keeps explicitly saved profiles; remove a selected profile separately to forget it.

What can’t this establish?

The tool does not authenticate a document or establish legal equivalence. It does not infer currency exchange rates, approve an order, contact a supplier, or change business records. Unreadable or ambiguous information remains visible. Workbook formulas use saved results; embedded charts and images in workbooks are outside the cell reader.

What do the five free attempts include?

Five live AI interpretations of uploaded document pairs per browser. Each admitted request uses one attempt, including a request the provider cannot finish. Clarifications, local recalculations, report downloads, and the recorded AI sample learning demonstration remain available. Anonymous browser identifiers cannot reliably identify a person across devices or cleared cookies; network and overall limits provide additional abuse protection.

Can I use handwritten paperwork?

Upload a clear photo or scanned PDF. Handwriting can be harder to read, especially crossed-out entries, cramped numbers, and dates. Review critical quantities, prices, part numbers, and dates against the original image; a plausible reading can still be wrong. You can correct the interpretation before comparing. Saved profiles remember form mappings and confirmed business rules; they do not guarantee recognition of a person’s handwriting.
